# 🛠️ How-To: Investigate Geopolitical Signals

This recipe covers investigating incoming real-time risk feeds, launching multi-agent debates, and generating compliance-verified strategic decision briefs.

---

## 1. Inspecting Live Signal Feeds
1. In the sidebar, select **Signal Center**.
2. Browse the incoming cards. Feeds are polled from Bloomberg, Reuters, FT, WSJ, and CoinDesk.
3. Click any card to expand full metadata, original article link, source timestamp, and extracted risk category.

---

## 2. Running a Manual Investigation Lab
To analyze an event or custom market hypothesis not present in the live RSS feed:
1. In the **Manual Investigation Lab** panel, enter a headline, scenario, or asset ticker (e.g. `OPEC announces surprise production cut amid Red Sea shipping delays`).
2. Click **"Run Causal Investigation"**.
3. The platform executes the following pipeline:
   - Queries the Dubstrata Causal Graph for downstream ripple effects and conflicting alt-data.
   - Generates a **Strategic Geopolitical Decision Brief** and a **B2B Outreach Draft**.
   - Evaluates copy against deterministic copywriting and compliance rules (whitespace, visceral verbs, no banned words).
   - Records an immutable SHA-256 chained entry into the audit ledger.
   - Automatically initializes an **Agent Hub** session and triggers the **Visionary** to provide initial analysis.
4. The generated assets appear immediately in the **Generated Content Stream** with status badges and full text copy.

---

## 3. Launching an Autonomous Subagent Debate
1. Navigate to **Agent Hub (Chat)**.
2. Select your newly spawned session (or type a custom topic and click **"New Session"**).
3. Toggle the **Auto-Pilot** switch to **ON**.
4. The system will:
   - Sequentially pass the floor between the 5 specialist agents with a 3-second pacing delay.
   - Produce real-time typing indicators in the UI.
   - Stream agent responses into the chat log with color-coded avatar badges and SOP codes (`SOP-STR-001`, `SOP-OPS-004`, etc.).
   - Allow agents to inspect and update sandbox CRM tables (`crm_accounts.csv`, `crm_opportunities.csv`).
5. To take manual command, toggle **Auto-Pilot** to **OFF** and click any individual agent button (e.g., **Summon Seller**), or type your message directly as the Operator.
